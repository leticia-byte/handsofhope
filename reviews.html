Facebook Review

Dr. Miller has been an absolute God-sent for our senior dog. Our boy developed severe pain and discomfort very rapidly, in the matter of days and progressed very quickly. He went from acting and playing like a young dog to not even being able to lift his head and yelping out in pain, all very suddenly. We went to one local vet who saw his age and diagnosed him with full-body arthritis and recommended injections that could have severe side effects. This DX didn’t sit right with me and I wanted a 2nd opinion. When I read about Hands of Hope offering services such as acupuncture, laser, and chiropractic services I decided to give them a try. Dr. Miller spent over an hour with us, giving a more definitive diagnosis that actually made sense and then provided an adjustment and laser session that same visit. My boy walked out of that initial visit with his head held high and even wagged his tail which he hadn’t done in weeks due to the pain. And every visit since she has continuously improved his pain to being pain-free in under 2 months. She’s given my boy a second wind at life. I 10000% recommend Dr. Miller and the services she provides. The staff is absolutely wonderful as well and so sweet and caring. I plan to switch to them for all of our vet needs.

— Mallory H.
